# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 10, 2026_

This application is a private, personal-use integration between Google Nest and a self-hosted Home Assistant system.

## Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the application may access Nest home and device metadata, thermostat state, sensor readings, and device events. It may send device commands only when initiated by an authorized user or an automation configured by that user.

## Use and storage

Data is used solely to display, automate, and control the owner's devices in Home Assistant. OAuth credentials and integration data are stored in the owner's private Home Assistant system. No data is sold, used for advertising, or intentionally shared with third parties.

## Retention and deletion

Data retention is controlled by the owner's Home Assistant configuration. The owner can remove the integration and its locally stored credentials at any time, and can revoke Google access through their Google Account's third-party connections settings.

## Contact

Questions may be sent to the user-support address displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
